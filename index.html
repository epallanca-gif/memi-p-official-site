01 — Biography

BEYOND
THE
BEAT.

MEMI P. has shaped a distinct space in house music through a refined sound, pioneering vision and an authentic devotion to the craft. His collaborations include some of the genre’s most defining voices: Michelle Weeks, SuSu Bobien, Dawn Tallman, Barbara Tucker, Deanna and Jisele Jackson.

He has also produced with William Naraine, the historic voice of Double You, pairing elegance with emotional force. His ambitious The House Divas project united five queens of house music on a single record — an anthem to femininity and the expressive power of the voice in dance music.

Commissioned by EMI Music, MEMI P. created the official remix of “Message in the Bottle” by The Police, reaffirming his ability to bring a contemporary edge to timeless records.
